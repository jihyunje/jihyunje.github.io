About

I am a first year student in the Masters of Science in Robotics Systems Development program at Carnegie Mellon University. Through my Masters program, I am learning industry-oriented systems engineering practices for taking a robotic system through the full development lifecycle. I am passionate about developing and deploying systems that make an impact on people's everyday lives, and am looking for Summer Internships where I can use my technical skills to do so.

With my coursework and research, I am gaining experience in developing state-of-the-art learning methods and applying them to real robotic systems. My personal interest is investigating how robots can live amongst and assist humans, especially how they can recognize human intent to provide proper assistance.

I recently graduated from California Institute of Technology with a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science. During my Bachelors, I refined my technical skills pertaining to robotics, including both software and mechanical skills. I've developed the full stack for multiple robotic systems, as can be seen in my Work section.
