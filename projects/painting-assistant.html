Human–robot interaction · Research in progress

Shared-control painting assistant

I'm developing shared-control robotic teleoperation to assist people with Parkinson's disease in painting, with a focus on preserving the user's intent and sense of agency.

The research

Research goal

Painting requires expressive motion, so assistance must support a person's intended stroke without taking away their control. This project studies how robotic guidance can provide that support.

My current work

I'm investigating intent-aware assistance with vision-language-action models. I'm also planning a formative user study and a dataset collection pipeline to identify user needs, evaluate sense of agency, and inform the shared-control strategy.

Status

The system and study are in development. Public demo media is not yet available.
